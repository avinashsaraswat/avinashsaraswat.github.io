Writing

A series on running knowledge work in an agentic IDE, and short takes on enterprise AI in regulated finance.

One piece every two weeks. Each piece makes one claim a practitioner can test, shows a receipt from a real run, and states a limitation. Demos come with a recording, and some pieces ship a template you can clone. Pieces and takes post on Substack, each with a LinkedIn post, and the recordings are embedded in both posts. Templates and demo vaults go on GitHub.

Dates are the week the piece is due. A piece is listed as published only once it is live, with its link.
