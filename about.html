About

I work on the infrastructure and deployment choices behind enterprise AI.

My work at Google Cloud spans AI infrastructure solutions, go-to-market initiatives, and partnerships. Before Google, I spent about nine years at EY on cloud and digital transformation in financial services, including supervisory remediation programs at large US banks.

My public work includes an article on AI Industry Bundles, coauthored with Fernando Rubbo. We also feature in the Google Cloud Next 2026 session on domain-specific models at scale with GKE.

I focus on how infrastructure, specialized models, and deployment choices fit the needs of an industry. The work page links the article and the session.

I write a series on running knowledge work in an agentic IDE, and each piece shows the run behind it. I also maintain a public evaluation harness for AI-generated financial analysis.
