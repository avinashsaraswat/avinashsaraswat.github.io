Code

fsi-eval-harness

A public evaluation harness for LLM output on regulated financial data. An LLM drafts an analyst-style brief from the structured XBRL facts of an SEC 10-Q or 10-K filing. A deterministic verifier checks every numeric GAAP claim against those facts. The brief is regenerated until each claim reconciles or is dropped. Every keep and drop is logged with a reason code. Python, Apache-2.0 license.
